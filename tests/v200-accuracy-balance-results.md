# v200 Level 10 accuracy balance

Seed: `0x1E6E0D`

Version 200 is an accuracy-meter-only balance pass based on the user's recorded Level 10 Pro round. Power timing, shot physics, wind, driver dispersion and the 439-yard Legend requirement remain unchanged.

Changes:

- Pro accuracy rate: 7.3 to 7.1 rad/s.
- Legend accuracy rate: 8.4 to 8.0 rad/s.
- Accuracy level pressure: 3% to 2% per level relative to the rank reference level.
- Power level pressure remains 3% per level.

At Level 10 Pro, the effective accuracy rate falls from 7.96 to 7.53 rad/s, a 5.4% reduction. Edge-to-edge travel increases from approximately 0.395 to 0.417 seconds. This adds useful input time without altering maximum distance or making mishits artificially straighter.
